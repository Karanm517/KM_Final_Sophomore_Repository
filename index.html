Karan Mehta

Student Developer • Programmer • Aspiring Game Designer

I am a junior at Thomas A. Edison High School with a strong passion for programming, technology, game development, and interactive digital experiences. Over the years, I have explored multiple areas of computer science, including Python programming, web development, application design, and software engineering. What started as simple curiosity has developed into a genuine passion for creating projects that combine creativity with technical problem-solving.

Through both coursework and independent learning, I have developed experience working with programming concepts such as algorithms, event-driven systems, user interface design, debugging, and iterative development. I enjoy transforming ideas into working applications and continuously improving them through testing, feedback, and refinement.

One of my primary interests is game design and development. I enjoy studying how games engage players through mechanics, progression systems, visual design, and interactive storytelling. Building projects has taught me the value of persistence, adaptability, and attention to detail while helping me strengthen my technical and creative skills.

My long-term goal is to pursue a career in software development and game design, creating applications and experiences that are both functional and engaging. I am constantly seeking opportunities to expand my knowledge, explore new technologies, and take on challenging projects that push my abilities as a developer.

Featured Project: DragonZ

DragonZ is a game concept and application development project that demonstrates my experience with planning, design, testing, and presentation. The project includes a playable demonstration and a detailed slideshow documenting the development process, design decisions, obstacles encountered, and improvements made throughout production.
