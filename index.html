Karan Mehta

Student Developer • Programmer • Aspiring Game Designer

I am a junior at Thomas A. Edison High School with a strong passion for programming, technology, and interactive digital experiences. Over the past several years, I have explored different areas of software development, including Python programming, web development, and game design. What started as a curiosity about how games and applications are built has grown into a genuine interest in creating my own projects and learning the tools used by professional developers.

Through coursework and independent projects, I have gained experience working with programming concepts such as problem-solving, algorithms, event-driven programming, and user interaction design. I enjoy taking an idea from the planning stage and turning it into something people can interact with, whether it is a game, simulation, or web application. Every project presents new challenges that help me strengthen both my technical and creative thinking skills.

One area that especially interests me is game development. I enjoy designing systems, creating engaging user experiences, and experimenting with different gameplay mechanics. Working on game-related projects has taught me the importance of iteration, testing, and continuous improvement. Throughout the development process, I focus on learning from mistakes, refining ideas, and finding creative solutions to technical challenges.

My goal is to continue expanding my programming knowledge while building increasingly sophisticated applications and games. As I progress through my education, I hope to further develop my skills in software engineering, game design, and interactive media while creating projects that demonstrate both technical ability and creativity.
